"[10 PRINT](https://10print.org/)" is a sort of _Hello, world_ of the generative arts world. I somehow was unaware of it even though I spent a bit of time building demos on the Commodore 64; probably this was because I learned BASIC on the Apple II where it wasn't as commonly referenced.

But later in life I started following the work of [Casey Reas](https://reas.com/) and he frequently starts introductory lectures with a reference to it. So when I wanted to try authoring a PICO-8 cartridge, it was natural to draw up a simple sprite and write 18 lines of code in honor of this [famous one-liner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-liner_program#BASIC).
