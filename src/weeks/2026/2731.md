I spend so much mental energy being quiet. Not abusing the power of my voice to quiet others. Reminding myself that not every question (or uptalk) is actually seeking an answer. Withholding observations that would unproductively cause discomfort. Keeping secrets that aren't mine to divulge.

It makes me worry about aging, and how I might behave when my self-control falters.
