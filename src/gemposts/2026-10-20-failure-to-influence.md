=> gemini://idiomdrottning.org/culpa Sandra feels guilty for "letting" the rest of us destroy our planet.

I feel this. I'm doing maybe 2.5 of her 4 things to opt out of the destruction: stopped eating meat ages ago, switched to plant-based milk, transport by foot, bike, and sometimes train. That .5 is because I do still fly, but far, far less than in the past. We recently took trains all the way from Amsterdam to the Romania-Ukraine border to avoid it.

Yet the failure to influence haunts me. I can flatter myself as someone who has inspired other people to take up sports and set career decisions at least partially due to my example or even push. Maybe I have been a small part of the few who have reduced their car dependance or air travel.

But, despite my gentle-enough-to-guide but not so-strong-as-to-alienate pushing, no one gives up meat. Even friends who acknowledge the ecological cost won't change behavior, and only a few even abstain in my presence.

WTF? You respect my opinion and leadership on even more personally difficult decisions but wave off this?

I don't know what we are to do besides keep trying, and maybe writing about this frustration publicly is a part of that.
