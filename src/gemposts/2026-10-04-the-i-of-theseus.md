=> gemini://gemini.madsweeney.me/articles/2026-09-19-the-ship-of-theseus-has-a-mind.gmi "The Ship of Theseus Has a Mind" from Mad Sweeney

I enjoyed this post and of course agree with their "conclusion" that the Ship of Theseus thought experiment is about the names we give "things", which are really patterns with discernible continuity through time.

But this is what sticks with me:
> The problem is that a brain, unlike a server, is the kind of thing that might have an inside.
My initial reaction was to tut about how our post-Descartes conception of consciousness fools us into thinking of selves as something _beyond_ the material from which it emerges. But I don't think that's what they're doing here. Without explicitly citing self construction, they've added subjectivity (in the philosophical sense) to the concept of emergence.

This is a helpful description of why it's easy to understand a "ship" an pattern in space and through time, but a lot harder to conceptualize an emergent self similarly. When we think about the self, we do so from "inside".
