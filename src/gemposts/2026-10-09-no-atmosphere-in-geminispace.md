title: No ATmosphere in Geminispace?
---


I notice a pattern that people with capsules share "Mastodon" Fediverse handles, but not "Bluesky" ATmosphere IDs.

I wonder if there is a cultural alignment, with Mastodon being much more "just us nerds" than Bluesky. Or maybe it's just an artifact of many capsules having been started when it was the only viable replacement for Twitter.

In either case, it saddens me a bit. I have come to appreciate atproto and am even tempted to experiment with gemproto-atproto bridging, but I think I might be the only one who would care to use it.
