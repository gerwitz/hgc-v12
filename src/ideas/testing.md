---
title: "Limit Testing"
---
When testing the resiliance or performance of a system, physical or software, you don't really know what its limit is until you've exceeded it.

This means the best tests will be destructive.

Similarly, when trying to evaluate a person, you have not found the level of their ability until they have failed.

If you take a test and never feel like it's too hard and you're failing, then it's not a well-designed test.
