For our 17th anniversary, we set out to one of the newest neighborhoods in walkng distance and visited the restaurand of [Lars Scharp and Floor Wiggers](https://www.missethoreca.nl/308720/lars-scharp-start-eigen-restaurant).

They are both quirky-charming and the kitchen is great. The food was all reliably good and felt unique. Maybe no single course stood out, but every one was a "keeper".

Unfortunately, the front of house was not so sharp. Service was unreliable and we spent too much time feeling forgotten. Most noticably, they seemed to lack a sommelier! When we splurge on such a culinary experience, we expect to have wine or non-alchoholic drink pairings that were designed along with the food.
