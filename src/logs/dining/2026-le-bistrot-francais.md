A proper coursed dining experience isn't easy to find in Bucharest, much less on a Tuesday and willing to server vegetarians. But I am happy Shannon and I found this place.

The food was good, expecially a deeply-vanilla dessert. The service was very attentive and wine pairings were very on-point, though they did re-use our glasses once or twice.

The venue is very charming. It's not likely I'll have the chance, but I'd be happy to revisit.
